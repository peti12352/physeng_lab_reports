R(T) measurement implemented with VISA/SCPI control for the Peltier supply, DAQ-based voltage/current readout, and PT1000 temperature conversion. One run was noisy after moving the setup; after redoing with stable wiring and contact, a clean heating--cooling hysteresis was observed and transition temperature aligned with expectations.

\section*{Task 4: Relaxation (Reset) Time}

\textbf{Devices:} VO$_2$ memristors at row 4, col 2 and row 4, col 3.\\
\textbf{Method:} Two-channel oscilloscope capture with 50~$\Omega$ termination; VIN(t) from AWG; V$_{\text{TRANS}}$(t) at the oscilloscope input. Compute $R_M(t)$ via $R_M(t)=2Z_0\left(\frac{\mathrm{VIN}(t)}{\mathrm{VTRANS}(t)}-1\right)$ with $Z_0=50\,\Omega$. Clear LRS$\to$HRS relaxation observed; reset time in the expected $\mu$s regime under our pulse/readout settings.

\textbf{Future analysis:} percentile-based time markers (e.g., 10--90\% of $\Delta R$) and stretched-exponential fits $R_M(t)=R_\infty-\Delta R\,e^{-(t/\tau)^\beta}$ to extract $\tau$ and $\beta$ vs. pulse amplitude and readout level.

\section*{Brief Error Notes}

Timing uncertainty from scope sampling/trigger; amplitude scale from 50~$\Omega$ tolerance and vertical calibration; residual reflections minimized with short cables and proper termination; thermal/mechanical stability improved repeatability.
